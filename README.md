## Business Situation Overview

Our group is modeling the operational infrastructure for a "Last-Mile" Urban Logistics company specializing in autonomous drone delivery. The company operates a network of regional hubs that house fleets of drones, charging stations, and maintenance personnel. The core operational challenge is managing rapid package deployment while monitoring drone battery life, flight hours, and maintenance safety requirements.

The database will track the physical delivery lifecycle from customer order to route dispatch alongside the critical backend logistics of fleet charging sessions and technician maintenance logs. To support these operations, the system must enforce strict rules regarding drone availability, technician credentials, and station capacity.
